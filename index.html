Wellspring

A personal video production tool for one creator's own YouTube channel.

Wellspring runs on the creator's own computer. It helps write, narrate and edit educational videos, which the creator reviews and approves before anything is published. It then uploads the approved videos to the creator's own YouTube channel and shows how the channel is doing.

It is not a public service: there are no accounts to sign up for, and it only ever connects to the YouTube channel of the person who runs it.

What it uses YouTube for

  • Uploading videos the creator has approved, with a scheduled publish time.
  • Setting each video's thumbnail.
  • Reading the channel's own statistics and analytics (subscribers, views, watch time).
  • Reading YouTube's public chart of popular Education videos, as inspiration for topics.

Videos made with AI voices or images are marked as altered or synthetic content when they are uploaded.
